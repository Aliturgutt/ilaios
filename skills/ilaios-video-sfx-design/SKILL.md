# ILAIOS Video SFX Design

Use this skill to specify intentional sound-effect cues tied to storyboard events and timeline positions.

## Canonical execution

Reuse canonical SOUND_EFFECT assets and `AudioProcessingCoordinator`. Keep cue purpose, source/provenance, start time, duration, gain intent, and collision/ducking requirements explicit.

## Boundaries

The skill does not download copyrighted audio, bypass source licensing, or perform uncontrolled filesystem/network access.
