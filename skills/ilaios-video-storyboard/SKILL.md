# ILAIOS Video Storyboard

Use this skill after a canonical VideoScript exists and before asset acquisition or generation.

## Canonical execution

Reuse `ScenePlanner` and canonical shot planning. Each beat must declare purpose, duration, visual description, narration reference, transition intent, and continuity-relevant state.

## Boundaries

No provider, asset downloader, renderer, or approval authority is introduced. The storyboard is planning evidence, not proof that media exists.
