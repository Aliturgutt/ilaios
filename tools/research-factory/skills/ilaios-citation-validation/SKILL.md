# ILAIOS Citation Validation
Status: IMPLEMENTED
Owner: ILAIOS

## Purpose
Check that each proposed research claim is bound to admitted evidence references and that the cited material is relevant to the stated claim.

## Authority
No direct network authority. This skill does not fetch missing sources, alter source provenance, choose providers, spend funds, approve itself, or publish externally.

## Output
A verification proposal identifying supported, unsupported, mismatched, missing, or ambiguous evidence references.
