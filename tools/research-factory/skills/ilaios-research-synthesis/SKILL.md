# ILAIOS Research Synthesis
Status: IMPLEMENTED
Owner: ILAIOS

## Purpose
Turn verified research evidence into a structured synthesis while preserving source references, uncertainty, and unresolved contradictions.

## Authority
No direct network authority. This skill cannot acquire new sources, override fact-check results, self-approve evidence, choose providers, retrieve secrets, or publish externally.

## Output
A knowledge-curation proposal bound to verified evidence. Unverified claims remain explicitly unverified and cannot be promoted by synthesis alone.
