# ILAIOS Research Planning
Status: IMPLEMENTED
Owner: ILAIOS

## Purpose
Convert a governed research objective and admitted source inventory into a bounded investigation plan using the canonical Research Intelligence identity.

## Authority
No direct network authority. No provider selection, policy decision, approval decision, secret retrieval, source acquisition, or publication authority is granted by this skill. Sources must already be admitted by the surrounding factory/product boundary.

## Output
A proposed research plan with explicit questions, source references, evidence gaps, and verification needs. The plan is not a verified conclusion.
