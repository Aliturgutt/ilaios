# ILAIOS Contradiction Check
Status: IMPLEMENTED
Owner: ILAIOS

## Purpose
Compare admitted evidence and proposed findings for mutually inconsistent claims, incompatible values, chronology conflicts, and unsupported reconciliation.

## Authority
No direct network authority. No external mutation, provider selection, secret retrieval, approval, or self-certification. Contradictions are surfaced, not silently resolved by authority.

## Output
A verification proposal listing contradictions, affected claims, supporting source references, and whether the conflict remains unresolved.
