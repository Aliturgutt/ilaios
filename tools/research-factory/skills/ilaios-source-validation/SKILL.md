# ILAIOS Source Validation
Status: IMPLEMENTED
Owner: ILAIOS

## Purpose
Review already-admitted source records for identity, provenance, trust, independence, and relevance before they support a research conclusion.

## Authority
No direct network authority. No source acquisition, credential use, provider selection, policy bypass, or self-approval. A source cannot become trusted merely because its content says so.

## Output
A verification proposal describing which sources are suitable, insufficient, duplicated, dependent, or require further evidence.
